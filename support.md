## Common Questions

**How do I delete my account?**
Open the App's Settings and choose Delete Account. This deletes your account and clears your Sunstreak data from your device. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for details.

**Why isn't the UV index showing?**
Sunstreak needs location access to show the UV index where you are. You can turn it on in your iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security, then Location Services.

**Why didn't I get a reapply reminder?**
Check that notifications are turned on for Sunstreak in your iPhone's Settings, and that Focus or Low Power Mode isn't holding them back. Reminders can still be delayed, so keep track of when you need to reapply yourself. See our [Health & Safety](health-and-safety.md) page.

**Is Sunstreak medical advice?**
No. Sunstreak is a habit tracker. For questions about your skin, talk to a healthcare provider or dermatologist.
